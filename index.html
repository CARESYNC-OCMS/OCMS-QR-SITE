PUP Taguig Online Clinic Management System

CareSync is the official website for the PUP Taguig Clinic. We made this page to help you understand your medical clearance steps, see when your course is scheduled for checkups, and submit your required health forms easily before you go to the campus.

Requirements to Bring During Medical Assessment

  • Printed copies and digital files of all your required documents
  • Your printed CareSync Health Information Form
  • Your completed handwritten Health Information Form (if you were asked to do one)
⚠️

IMPORTANT REMINDER

Please pass both your printed and handwritten health forms at the clinic so the doctor can verify them and update your records cleanly.

📋 Notice: Tell Us About Your Health History

Please make sure you list down all current and past illnesses or health conditions on your online form. The clinic staff will double-check everything and interview you about your answers during your visit.

⚠️ HEALTH RECORDS NOTE: If you have a history of chronic or past illnesses, you must bring your original medical documents along with clear photocopies for the clinic to file away safely.
